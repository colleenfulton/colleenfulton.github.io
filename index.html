Perspective

What is complex to build does not have to be complex to use. I started as a mechanical engineer, and designing how people experience a product changed how I built it. Engineering and design are two sides of the same coin, and I make decisions informed by both.

Approach

I refine technical systems into intuitive user workflows. I study how people use a product, find where they struggle, and revise until the system absorbs the complexity instead of the user.
